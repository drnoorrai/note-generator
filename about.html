About Oval Health

Clinical tools built for speed, clarity and trust.

Oval Health is a clinician workflow platform for structured documentation, patient information, clinical prompts and calculators.

Broad clinical fit

The product is GP-first but not GP-only. The system is designed to expand across primary care, emergency medicine, psychiatry, acute medicine and other clinical settings.

Safety notice

Oval Health tools are educational aids. Clinicians remain responsible for clinical decisions, documentation, prescribing, escalation and following local guidance.

Oval Health is an educational clinical workflow project. It does not replace professional judgement, local policy or current clinical guidance.
